# Psi

## Register

product

## Users

Sebastian and developers running extended AI coding and research loops for Battlecode competitions on a local computer. They move between source code, build tools, benchmark processes, website data and saved results, and need direct control over accounts and inference cost.

## Product Purpose

A personal, lightweight AI harness with a clean terminal interface, multiple accounts across providers, persistent autonomous work, and unrestricted local tool execution. Useful progress, recoverable evidence and low overhead matter more than decorative presentation.

## Brand Personality

Compact, understated, efficient. The Greek capital psi, Ψ, is the identity. Copy is brief and concrete.

## Anti-references

Unrequested ambient animation, busy dashboards, oversized tool output, nested account menus and interruption-heavy approval flows.

## Design Principles

- Keep the user in the terminal with searchable pickers and slash commands.
- Make active work, account choices and resource use understandable at a glance.
- Keep full evidence on disk and show concise previews in the conversation.
- Preserve progress through long runs and interruptions.
- Execute authorized local tools without permission dialogs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-first operation, clear text, standard terminal scrolling and a readable narrow layout. All status colors have text labels. No ambient animation; the explicitly requested 3D logo is opt-in via click or `/logo` and can be dismissed immediately. Inherit the user's terminal font, palette and background. No additional accessibility needs were specified.
